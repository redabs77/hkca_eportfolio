Tier 1 — anonymous trainer forms

Each supervising trainer completes the College’s 15-item In-Training Assessment form on their own (open the trainer’s form). Forms are read by the SOT and ASOT only, and without the trainer’s name. They are never shown to the trainee or to the College: the College sees only the final sign-off and your summary of comments. Nothing is pre-filled from earlier cycles. Tier 2 — SOT synthesis for the face-to-face meeting

Read the spread, not an average. For each of the 15 items you record the grade that goes on the College form. Where trainers differ by two or more levels you must record why. The overall assessment is yours to set — except that the College rule applies: overall is unsatisfactory if, but not limited to, 4 or more of the 15 items are unsatisfactory.

Mandatory face-to-face feedback meeting

Counter-signature happens in person, with the SOT and trainee reviewing the synthesis together. The trainee sees the overall assessment, the 15 item grades and the SOT’s comments — never an individual trainer’s form. If the trainee disputes the grade, the record moves to contested and the rotation credit is held pending BOE review.

A ledger grant, amendment or revocation is recorded as an immutable entry with the BOE actor ID and reason taxonomy. Nothing is deleted; corrections are posted as new rows.

Why this stays manual
No automated scoring or AI inference is permitted to set or amend a summative grade. The system presents the distribution, the divergence flags and the evidence — the adjudication is a human, panel-level decision recorded against a named BOE actor.
