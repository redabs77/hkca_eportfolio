2-tier ITA model: Tier 1 is each trainer's private, un-prefilled form on the College's 15 feedback items (ItaAssessorResponse; X/1/2/3/4, with an example required for a 4 only), visible only to the SOT and the College. Tier 2 is the SOT's consolidated grade for each of the 15 items plus the overall assessment (ItaSession.sotGrade), which the trainee counter-signs. The overall is unsatisfactory (4) when 4 or more items are graded 4; X items are not counted and a 3 is a pass. A trainee may contest the Tier 2 result, opening a formal ItaAppeal for BOE review. A second consecutive unsatisfactory result escalates automatically to the Board of Education (SOT → Assistant SOT → Cluster SOT → BOE).

Velocity risk projects whether a trainee's current requirement pace will clear their next mandatory gate on time — time remaining to the gate deadline vs. projected completion date for VOP/WBA/MSF at current rate, with peer pace shown as supporting context only. Assessment risk is the existing ITA-grade ladder (contested grade, 2 consecutive unsatisfactory → BOE, active remedial plan). A trainee can carry either, both, or neither.
