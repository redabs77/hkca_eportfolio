Tier 1 — trainer forms

Each supervising trainer completes the College’s 15-item In-Training Assessment form on their own (open the trainer’s form). Forms are read by the SOT and ASOT only, without the trainer’s name unless they agreed to be contacted. They are never shown to the trainee or to the College: the College sees only the final sign-off and your summary of comments. Nothing is pre-filled from earlier cycles. You are viewing one form at a time; trainers are named only where they agreed to be contacted.

Tier 2 — SOT synthesis for the face-to-face meeting

Read the spread, not an average. For each of the 15 items you record the grade that goes on the College form. Where trainers differ by two or more levels you must record why. The overall assessment is yours to set — except that the College rule applies: overall is unsatisfactory if, but not limited to, 4 or more of the 15 items are unsatisfactory.
