    Two independent risk signals, shown separately. Velocity risk projects whether a trainee's current requirement pace will clear their next mandatory gate on time — time remaining to the gate deadline vs. projected completion date for VOP/WBA/MSF at current rate, with peer pace shown as supporting context only. Assessment risk is the existing ITA-grade ladder (contested grade, 2 consecutive unsatisfactory → BOE, active remedial plan). A trainee can carry either, both, or neither. Both the SOT and the trainee see their own risk card.
