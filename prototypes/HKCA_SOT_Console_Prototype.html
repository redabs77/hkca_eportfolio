2-tier ITA model: Tier 1 is the private, un-prefilled trainer scoring layer (ItaAssessorResponse) — domain ratings, notable strengths and growth areas, visible only to the SOT. Tier 2 is the official, summative SOT grade (ItaSession.sotGrade) that the trainee counter-signs. A trainee may contest the Tier 2 grade, opening a formal ItaAppeal for BOE review. Second consecutive unsatisfactory Tier 2 grade escalates automatically to the Board of Education (SOT → Assistant SOT → Cluster SOT → BOE).
